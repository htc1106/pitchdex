Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25th, 2026

Pitchdex ("Pitchdex," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Cactus Marketing Inc. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use the Pitchdex platform and related services.

1. Who We Are

Pitchdex is an outbound email platform designed to help founders find and reach relevant venture capitalists and manage fundraising outreach.

Pitchdex / Cactus Marketing Inc.
179 George Street
Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
Email: hello@cactusmarketing.io

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password and login information
  • Connected email account information through Google
  • Email campaign data
  • Uploaded contact lists
  • VC contact data
  • Outreach history
  • Engagement data such as opens, clicks, replies, and bounces
  • Account notes
  • Integration data
  • Billing-related information
  • Support messages and communications

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information:

  • directly from you when you create an account or use the platform
  • through integrations you connect to Pitchdex
  • from imported or uploaded data
  • through your use of the platform and related outreach features

4. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account
  • provide the Pitchdex platform and its features
  • send and manage outbound email campaigns
  • store and display outreach history
  • track campaign performance, including opens, clicks, replies, and bounces
  • process billing and payments
  • provide customer support
  • improve platform functionality and analytics
  • maintain platform security and integrity

5. Connected Accounts and Email Functionality

If you connect your account through Google or other supported integrations, we may access and process the information necessary to enable email sending, outreach management, and related platform functionality.

We store relevant outreach history and campaign performance data to support your ongoing use of the service.

6. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe. We do not store full payment card details ourselves unless expressly stated otherwise. Payment information is handled according to the applicable third-party provider's privacy practices.

7. Data Storage and Security

We store necessary account and platform data using AWS and take reasonable measures to protect personal information.

We use encryption and other reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies for advertising or non-essential tracking based on the information currently provided. We may use essential technical mechanisms necessary for platform operation, authentication, security, and performance.

9. Data Retention

We retain user data, including account and platform-related data, even if a subscription is no longer active, in order to preserve the account and information in case the user returns to the platform.

We may retain certain information where required for legal, regulatory, security, dispute resolution, or legitimate business purposes.

10. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers and partners that help us operate the platform, including providers for:

  • cloud hosting and infrastructure
  • payment processing
  • email and integration functionality
  • support and operational services

We may also disclose information:

  • if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • to enforce our terms or protect our rights
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets

11. International Processing

Your information may be stored or processed outside of Canada, including through third-party providers such as AWS, Stripe, and Google. By using Pitchdex, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in other jurisdictions that may have different data protection laws than your home jurisdiction.

12. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • update certain account information through the platform
  • contact us at hello@cactusmarketing.io with privacy-related requests
  • delete your account through the in-app settings, where available

Depending on your location, you may also have additional privacy rights under applicable law.

13. Children's Privacy

Pitchdex is intended for founders and is not directed to children.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the effective date above and take other appropriate steps to notify users where required.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to make a privacy-related request, please contact:
