Module 02

The Cold Email Platform

Generic outreach gets ignored. Pitchdex's email engine writes personalized first lines using each investor's recent investments, blog posts, and thesis, then sends them on a schedule that maximizes opens.

✓
Sequence builder: Drag-and-drop your initial email, follow-up 1, and follow-up 2. Timing fully configurable.
✓
AI personalization: Unique first lines for every email based on each investor's public profile and recent activity.
✓
Send from your domain: Emails go out from you@yourcompany.com. Not a shared IP. Not a no-reply address.
✓
Domain warmup: We gradually build your sending reputation so you land in the inbox, not spam.
✓
Reply detection: The moment a VC replies, their sequence pauses automatically. No awkward follow-ups after a yes.
✓
Analytics dashboard: Track opens, clicks, replies, and meetings booked by sequence, by investor, and over time.
